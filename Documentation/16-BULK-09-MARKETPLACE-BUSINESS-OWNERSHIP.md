# Bulk 09 - Marketplace and business ownership

This bulk implements the next master-spec commerce layers.

## Added
- Seller profiles and marketplace listings.
- Shopping cart/order state.
- Checkout foundation.
- Warehouses and delivery jobs.
- Delivery assignment/progress state.
- Returns/refunds/dispute-compatible order states.
- Seller disputes.
- Business registration/ownership state.
- Employee records and salaries.
- Supplier contracts.
- Profit/loss period ledger.
- Marketplace and business runtime contracts.
- Backend local marketplace/business services, routes and tests.
- Reference persistence tables.

## Master-spec alignment
Phase 21 specifies physical stores, supermarkets, markets, online marketplace, product search, cart, checkout, reviews, sellers, producer profiles, warehouses, deliveries, returns, refunds, seller disputes, Nigeria/Ghana producer balance and cross-border sourcing.

Phase 22 specifies business registration, premises, employees, salaries, inventory, suppliers, pricing, advertising, taxes, profit/loss, branches, expansion, bankruptcy, competition and reputation.

## Production boundary
This is simulation architecture, not real commerce. Prices, taxes, refunds, seller ratings and business values are fictional game-balance data. Payment processing remains in-game simulation only.
