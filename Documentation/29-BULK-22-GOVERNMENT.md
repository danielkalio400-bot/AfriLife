# Bulk 22 - Government foundation

This bulk implements Phase 35 of the AfriLife master specification as politically neutral fictional simulation state.

## Added
- President, minister, governor, legislator and agency-director officeholder state.
- Government agencies.
- Legislature measures and neutral vote tallies.
- Budgets and approval state.
- Public projects.
- Public salary schedules.
- Treasury state.
- Public-spending records with budget caps.
- Public audits.
- Procurement workflow.
- Fictional election records and fictional-party candidates.
- Public-approval metrics.
- Government-service capacity/availability.
- Backend routes/tests and persistence tables.

## Master-spec alignment
Phase 35 lists President, Ministers, Governors, Legislature, Agencies, Budgets, Public projects, Salaries, Treasury, Public spending, Audits, Procurement, Elections, Public approval and Government services.

## Neutrality boundary
No real Nigerian or Ghanaian politician, party or candidate is represented or endorsed. Election mechanics use fictional people/parties and deterministic neutral recordkeeping only.

## Production boundary
This does not model real constitutional law, real government databases, real procurement rules, official public-finance figures, real election procedures or current officeholders.
