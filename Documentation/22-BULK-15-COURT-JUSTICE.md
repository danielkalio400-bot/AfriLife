# Bulk 15 - Court and justice foundation

This bulk implements Phase 28 of the AfriLife master specification as a fictional administrative justice simulation.

## Added
- Judges, lawyers, prosecutors, clerks and witnesses as case participants.
- Evidence attachment.
- Traffic, criminal, civil and family court categories.
- Property/business dispute-compatible case subjects.
- Court scheduling.
- Court decisions.
- Appeals.
- Fine, restitution, community-service and custodial-sentence state.
- Prison handoff represented only as custodial-days state for the later prison phase.
- Backend court services/routes/tests and persistence tables.

## Master-spec alignment
Phase 28 lists judges, lawyers, prosecutors, clerks, witnesses, evidence, traffic/criminal/civil/family courts, property disputes, business disputes, appeals, sentencing, fines, restitution, community service and prison.

## Safety and scope boundary
This is fictional game state, not legal advice. Sentencing is arbitrary game-balance data and not a statement about Nigerian or Ghanaian law. No legal-evasion guidance or graphic prison content is included.

## Production boundary
There is no real court-data integration, legal document generation, real procedural-law engine or jurisdiction-specific sentencing model.
