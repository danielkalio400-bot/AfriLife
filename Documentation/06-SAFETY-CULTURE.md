# Safety and Cultural Boundaries

Crime, cybercrime, conflict, illegal substances and harmful ritual themes may exist as abstract simulation, story and consequence systems, but must not teach real methods for hacking, weapon construction, evasion, drug production, dangerous use or harmful rituals.

Real religions, ethnic traditions, ancestors and deities are treated respectfully and are not automatically portrayed as evil. Horror threats should be fictionalized or clearly folklore-inspired.

Adult relationships are limited to adult characters and intimate activity remains off-screen. Political simulation remains neutral; fictional parties are preferred unless rights and legal review support real branding.
