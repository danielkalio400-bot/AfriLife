# Bulk 16 - Prison system foundation

This bulk implements Phase 29 from the AfriLife master specification as a non-graphic correctional and reintegration simulation.

## Added
- Correctional facility capacity and intake.
- Cell assignment state.
- Meal-state foundation.
- Recreation, work, education, rehabilitation and health-care programs.
- Family and lawyer visits.
- Health-care requests.
- Sentence-day/time-compression state.
- Parole/release review.
- Release workflow.
- Reintegration plans for housing, employment, education, health and family support.
- Backend correctional services/routes/tests and persistence tables.

## Master-spec alignment
Phase 29 lists intake, cells, meals, recreation, work, education, health care, family visits, lawyer visits, rehabilitation, parole/release, time compression and reintegration.

## Safety and scope boundary
The implementation is deliberately non-graphic and does not include prison violence, escape methods, security-bypass tactics, self-harm content or real correctional-security procedures.

## Production boundary
This is fictional game state. It does not model real Nigerian or Ghanaian correctional law, prison operating procedures or parole rules.
