# Bulk 25 - News and media foundation

This bulk implements Phase 39 of the AfriLife master specification.

## Added
- Fictional radio outlets.
- Fictional television outlets.
- Fictional newspapers.
- Fictional social-feed outlets.
- Emergency-alert state.
- Breaking-news items.
- Government-announcement topic.
- Neutral election-coverage topic.
- Fuel-shortage news.
- Blackout news.
- Accident news.
- Crime news.
- Conflict/war news at a non-graphic level.
- Peace-talk coverage.
- Currency news.
- Broadcast scheduling.
- Country/topic feed filtering.
- Client DTOs, persistence, routes and tests.

## Master-spec alignment
Phase 39 lists radio, TV, newspapers, social feeds, emergency alerts, breaking news, government announcements, election coverage, fuel shortages, blackouts, accidents, crime, war, peace talks and currency news.

## Reality and neutrality boundary
All outlets and reports created by this subsystem are fictional in-world content. Political coverage is neutral and does not represent endorsement of any real person or party. Conflict/crime reports remain non-graphic.

## Production boundary
No real Nigerian or Ghanaian news feed, broadcaster, social network, emergency-alert network or government communications system is connected.
